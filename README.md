# 📊 Online Shopping Behavior Among University Students

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the online shopping behavior of university students at the Faculty of Natural Sciences, Can Tho University.

The study was conducted using survey data collected from **210 students**. The analysis explores students' online shopping behavior, preferred shopping platforms, monthly spending, shopping barriers, satisfaction, and relationships between selected variables.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

This project aims to:

- Describe the online shopping behavior of university students.
- Analyze online shopping frequency and monthly spending.
- Identify the most commonly used online shopping platforms.
- Examine factors and barriers related to online shopping.
- Analyze the relationship between monthly income and online shopping expenditure.
- Examine whether monthly online shopping expenditure differs by gender.

---

## 📊 Dataset

- **Sample size:** 210 students
- **Population:** Students at the Faculty of Natural Sciences, Can Tho University
- **Data collection method:** Survey questionnaire
- **Data format:** SPSS `.sav`

---

## 🛠 Tools & Methods

### Tool
- SPSS

### Statistical Methods
- Descriptive Statistics

# 📈 Key Findings

## 🛒 Online Shopping Platforms

The most commonly used online shopping platforms were:

| Platform | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Shopee | 97.1% |
| TikTok Shop | 62.4% |
| Facebook | 45.7% |
| Instagram | 23.3% |
| Lazada | 21.0% |

Shopee was the most commonly reported online shopping platform among the surveyed students.

---

## 💰 Monthly Online Shopping Spending

| Monthly Spending | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Below 500,000 VND | 41.9% |
| 500,000–1,000,000 VND | 33.3% |
| 1,000,000–1,500,000 VND | 13.3% |
| Above 1,500,000 VND | 11.4% |

Most respondents reported spending less than **1,000,000 VND per month** on online shopping.

---

## 📊 Income and Online Shopping Spending

A Pearson correlation analysis was conducted to examine the relationship between monthly income and monthly online shopping expenditure.

- **Correlation coefficient:** r = 0.159
- **p-value:** p = 0.021

The analysis found a weak positive relationship between income and monthly online shopping expenditure in the surveyed sample.

---

## 👥 Gender and Monthly Spending

An Independent Samples T-Test was conducted to compare monthly online shopping expenditure between male and female students.

- **t = 1.183**
- **df = 208**
- **p = 0.238**

At the 0.05 significance level, the analysis did not find sufficient statistical evidence of a difference in mean monthly online shopping expenditure between male and female respondents.

---

## 🔬 Statistical Analysis

### Chi-Square Test

Chi-Square tests were used to examine the association between gender and the use of selected online shopping platforms.

The analysis found a statistically significant association for **Facebook usage** at the 0.05 significance level.

---

### Pearson Correlation

The analysis examined the relationship between:

> Monthly Income ↔ Monthly Online Shopping Expenditure

Result:

> **r = 0.159, p = 0.021**

---

### Independent Samples T-Test

The analysis compared monthly online shopping expenditure between:

> Male students ↔ Female students

Result:
